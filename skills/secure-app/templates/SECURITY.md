# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately to **<security contact email or private reporting link>**. Do not open a public issue.

Include: what you found, how to reproduce it, the affected version, and any impact you have observed. Do not access other people's data, degrade the service, or keep copies of data you encounter.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within **<3> working days**.
- We give a first assessment within **<10> working days** and keep you informed.
- We aim to fix confirmed high-severity issues within **<30> days** and credit reporters who want it.

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| <current> | yes |

| older | no |

In scope: <the application, its API, official builds>. Out of scope: <third-party services, social engineering, denial of service by volume>.
